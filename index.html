RESEARCH & THINGS I BUILD

I'm an undergraduate at Carleton College studying Computer Science and Mathematics. I'm interested in AI for scientific discovery and in building agent systems that can turn ideas into experiments.

I work with Prof. Aryan Deshwal at the University of Minnesota on AI agents for algorithm development, using molecular design as a testing ground. I began this collaboration during a summer research internship in 2026.

I'm also building Cleo, a local-first AI workspace, and contributing to digital scholarship at Carleton, where I developed the Historical Maps plugin for the Carleton Guide to Medieval Rome.

Previously, I worked on RNA 3D structure prediction and medical computer vision.

RNA 3D structure prediction

January–April 2025

Predicting 3D nucleotide coordinates directly from RNA sequences with a hybrid CNN–Transformer model. I explored local geometric structure and long-range dependencies under limited data and compute, using residue-level supervision masks and length-aware batching.
